Federal Socioeconomic Designations

How we qualify for set-asides

Service-Disabled Veteran-Owned Small Business (SDVOSB)

US Government Services LLC is a 100% veteran-owned small business and a Service-Disabled Veteran-Owned Small Business with an application for SBA VetCert certification under 13 C.F.R. Part 128 currently in process. Our managing member, LTC John Michael Stacy, USA (Ret.), holds a 100% permanently and totally service-connected disability rating from the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs.

Per SBA's 2024 final rule, only firms certified by SBA VetCert may be awarded SDVOSB set-aside or sole-source contracts. We will update this page and post our certification link the moment VetCert is granted.

Small Business

US Government Services LLC qualifies as a small business under the SBA size standards for each of our registered NAICS codes.

VA Veterans First Eligible

Upon SBA VetCert certification, US Government Services LLC will be eligible for VA "Veterans First" priority under 38 U.S.C. § 8127, including sole-source services awards up to $5 million per VAAR 819.7008.

Contracting Pathways

Three ways to put us on your next requirement

Even before our VetCert certification finalizes, there are clear, immediate ways to engage us.

1

SDVOSB Sole-Source (upon certification)

Once SBA VetCert is granted, you may award services contracts up to $5 million on a sole-source basis under FAR 19.1406. The most efficient award vehicle in federal procurement — no competition required.

2

VA Veterans First (upon certification)

Once certified, services contracts may be awarded sole-source under VAAR 819.7008 up to $5 million, applying the Rule of Two confirmed by the Supreme Court in Kingdomware v. United States.

3

Teaming & Subcontracting (available now)

We actively pursue teaming arrangements and subcontracting roles under prime IDIQs. Available immediately — including SBA Mentor-Protégé candidacy and prime supplier-diversity registrations.

Vehicles we are pursuing: GSA Multiple Award Schedule (Startup Springboard program); VA T4NG II; VECTOR; GSA VETS 2 GWAC (upon eligibility).
